# Apen Super App R3 — product summary

Apen is one offline business-records app for Inuka Africa clients who earn from dairy farming, rental property, food businesses or small shops. It gives each business its own home screen and working vocabulary, while using shared accounts, expenses, summaries, history, backup and reporting. An owner who runs two businesses can switch between them in the same APK without mixing their records.

This is a review application. It is a proposed customer tool, not an Inuka banking portal or a verified source of credit information. Customer release readiness and validation results are stated separately in the R3 handover.

## Who it serves

- A smallholder dairy farmer selling to a society, processor, broker, hotel or neighbour.
- A small landlord recording monthly rent for occupied and vacant units.
- A roadside restaurant, food kiosk or similar business that needs quick sales and regular-customer Deni.
- A small shopkeeper managing items, stock, part payments and later debt collections.
- An owner with more than one of these businesses on the same phone.

## What the four scenarios do

| Scenario | Day-to-day capabilities | Practical business benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Dairy | Record morning/evening milk deliveries, litres and price; maintain buyer accounts; record payments and cost/non-cost deductions; record feed, vet and other costs; review litres, value and buyer balances. | Makes it easier to compare delivered milk with buyer payments, follow up unpaid amounts and see recorded operating costs. |
| Landlord | Maintain properties, units and tenancies; create monthly charges; track arrears and vacancy; allocate payments to oldest charges; preview rent receipts and reminders before opening the phone's share sheet. | Reduces reliance on memory when following up rent, identifying part payments and carrying arrears between months. |
| Restaurant | Use large menu tiles and direct Cash/M-Pesa/Deni actions; record regular-customer tabs and collections; record shopping and other expenses; see sales and top-selling items. | Supports quicker counter entry and clearer daily records of sales, unpaid tabs and shopping. |
| Retail | Use item names, prices and quantities; see stock left; add stock/restock; record part-paid sales and later collections; use a dedicated Deni tab. | Helps the owner follow stock and unpaid customer balances without counting a collection as another sale. |

## Shared capabilities

- Several separate business profiles in one installation, with a clearly marked practice/demo mode.
- English and Kiswahili interfaces, business-specific colours and illustrations, large text and controls.
- Local owner sign-in/PIN and encrypted whole-app backup/recovery covering every business and personal loan note.
- Stable record references, activity dates, original entry times where known, and reasoned corrections/voids with before/after history. Legacy entry times remain unknown when they were not recorded.
- Daily, weekly, monthly and custom-period summaries. Missing dates remain unknown; equal-period comparisons require sufficient recorded coverage.
- One business-statement format for all four types, with preview, explicit consent, PDF saving/sharing and versioned CSV saving. Statements include definitions, coverage, report identity, generation time and app version, and omit individual party details and personal notes.
- Loans and plans remain information/personal-reference features. The app does not submit, approve or service a loan.

## What its numbers mean

Milk earned, shop/food sales and rent charges are different from money received. A later collection reduces an existing debt and does not create a new sale. Capital, grants, transfers and loan proceeds are separate from sales. Deposits are recorded as held money rather than income; deposit refunds are not automatically reconciled.

Sales/earned less recorded expenses is not full profit or a cash/wallet balance. It excludes unrecorded costs and, for traded goods, historical cost of goods sold. Rental summaries separately describe collected money less recorded expenses. Outstanding amounts are labelled as the balance of all currently posted records across dates, rather than a historical period-end balance.

For example, the dairy acceptance case records 43 litres at KES 50, giving KES 2,150 earned. After KES 800 recorded costs, the difference is KES 1,350 before unrecorded costs. KES 50 remains owed by the buyer. This distinction helps the owner see what was earned, what was received and what remains unpaid.

## Objectives and limits

The immediate objective is consistent everyday record keeping in one maintainable app. Clearer records can support the owner's own decisions and a discussion with Inuka, subject to the lender's processes. The app cannot establish that a self-entered transaction really happened, assess creditworthiness, guarantee business growth or promise finance. It does not confirm M-Pesa payments, reconcile a bank balance, automatically send records to Inuka, or provide cloud synchronisation.

Poultry is the next planned independent scenario. It will reuse the shared foundation while giving flocks, eggs and bird sales their own records and flows. It remains outside R3 until the current iteration passes review; see the separate Poultry scenario plan.
