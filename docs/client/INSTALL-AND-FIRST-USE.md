# FasMova POS - Install and first use

FasMova POS runs on your Android phone or tablet and works without internet. Everything you record is stored **on that device only**, so please read "Protect your data" below.

## 1. Install the app (about 2 minutes)

You received a file named like `FasMovaPOS-1.0.apk`.

1. Copy the file to the phone (WhatsApp, email, USB cable, or memory card) and open it from the **Downloads** or **Files** app.
2. If Android says *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*, tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this source**, then go back and tap **Install**.
3. If *Play Protect* shows a warning that the app is from an unknown developer, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. This message appears for every app installed outside the Play Store.
4. Tap **Open**.

To check you have the genuine file, compare its fingerprint with the one we sent you: in a file manager that supports it, or ask us for help. (Fingerprint: see the message that came with the file.)

## 2. First start: set up your business (about 1 minute)

The first time you open the app it asks for:

- **Business name**: appears on your receipts and at the top of the screen.
- **Your full name** and a **username** for signing in (for example `owner`).
- **A 4-digit PIN** that only you know. Not `1234`. You type it twice.
- **Add sample data for training** *(optional)*: fills the app with example products and customers so you can practise. Leave it **off** for a real shop.

Tap **Start**. You are signed in as the Owner.

## 3. Add your staff

Open **Staff** and add each person with a username and a temporary PIN. The first time they sign in, the app makes them choose their own private PIN before they can do anything else.

## 4. Take your first sale

1. Open **POS**, tap products to add them to the cart.
2. Choose how the customer pays and tap **Pay**.
3. **Mobile money (M-Pesa or Airtel Money):** the customer pays to your till or number, receives an SMS with a confirmation code (for example `QKJ9821LXM`). Type that code into the app. The app refuses a code that has already been used for another sale.
4. The receipt appears.

> The app does **not** talk to Safaricom or Airtel yet, so it cannot check that the money arrived. Always look at the SMS on your own phone before releasing the goods.

## 5. Protect your data

Your sales, stock and customers live only on this phone. If it is lost, stolen or broken, they are gone unless you made a backup.

- Open **Backup**, tap **Create backup**, choose a password, then pick where to save the file (Google Drive, WhatsApp to yourself, or a memory card).
- **Do this at the end of every week**, and always before you update the app or change phones.
- **Remember your backup password.** Nobody, including us, can open a backup without it.
- To move to a new phone, install the app there, and on the first screen (or in **Backup**) choose **Restore from backup**.
- **Export sales (spreadsheet)** gives your accountant a file for Excel or Google Sheets.

Only the **Owner** can back up and restore.

## 6. Updating the app

When we send you a new `.apk`, install it over the old one the same way. Your data stays. **Make a backup first.**

## 7. Language and appearance

Use the buttons at the top of the sign-in screen (and in the app) to switch between English and Kiswahili, and between light and dark.
